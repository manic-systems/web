NotAShelf, better known as "raf", is a professional sailor and sailmaker, a
political scientist, and a long-time contributor to the Nix/OS ecosystem. His
research focuses on how political institutions can meet everyday users where
they are on data protection and privacy, and the same concern shapes much of his
software.

Beyond his work on [Nixpkgs], raf maintains and contributes to projects
including:

- [nh], a Nix CLI helper that replaces and streamlines the core tooling
- [Hjem], a module system for managing your `$HOME`
- [nvf], a distro-agnostic framework for configuring Neovim
- [ndg], a documentation generator for Nix-based module systems
- [nixos-core], a Rust replacement for NixOS boot and activation scripts
- [evix], an asynchronous Nix evaluation engine
- [dix], a tool for comparing Nix closures and snapshots

More of his projects are documented on [his website](https://notashelf.dev/projects).

Outside of Nix, he writes small, sharp command-line tools in Rust, Go and C, and
keeps a quiet interest in DevSecOps and privacy hardening.

When he is not on the water or at the keyboard, raf writes about software, Linux
and Nix/OS, and spends his time on farming [^1] and carpentry.
